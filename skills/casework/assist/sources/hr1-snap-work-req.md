# H.R. 1 — SNAP work requirements (vetted summary, synthetic for demo)

Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents (ABAWDs) are subject to a weekly work-requirement threshold to maintain SNAP eligibility.

> "An individual is exempt from the SNAP work requirement if they are responsible for a dependent child under age 18 in their household."

> "Individuals who are physically or mentally unable to work, and those caring for an incapacitated household member, are exempt."

Hours that cannot be verified do not count toward the requirement; the agency should request documentation before any adverse action.
